Frequently Asked Questions

What is CropCare?

CropCare is an AI-powered agricultural decision support system that helps farmers make informed decisions about crop selection, disease diagnosis, and farming practices.

Is CropCare free to use?

Yes, CropCare offers a free tier with core features. Premium features are available through subscription plans.

How accurate are the crop recommendations?

Our recommendations are based on advanced algorithms and agricultural research. However, farming outcomes depend on various environmental factors.

Can I use CropCare offline?

Basic features require internet connectivity. We're working on offline capabilities for future releases.

How do I get support?

Contact our support team through the Help Center or email us at support@croppcare.com

After creating your account, complete your farm profile to receive personalized recommendations. Add your location, soil type, and land size for the best results.

Select your crop and variety to generate a detailed farming schedule. The planner provides daily tasks and input requirements based on your land size.

Upload clear photos of affected plants and describe symptoms for AI-powered diagnosis. Follow the treatment recommendations provided.
