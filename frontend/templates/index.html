About CropCare

Founded: 2026

CropCare is a revolutionary agricultural technology company dedicated to empowering farmers with AI-powered decision support systems. Founded by a team of agricultural scientists and technology experts, we believe that every farmer deserves access to cutting-edge tools that can transform their farming practices.

To democratize agricultural intelligence and make data-driven farming accessible to every farmer, regardless of scale or location.
